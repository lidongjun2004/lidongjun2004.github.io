- 邀请

  - 参与

    - 大型集体活动

      - participate in

      - take part in

    - join in an acitivity

    - join sb./an organization

    - 开会/上课

      - attend

  - 写作目的（邀请）

    - I'm writing with delight to invite you to join/participate in/take part in/attend/…… with me.

  - 活动内容

    - 地点

      - XXX will be held in 地点

    - 时间

      - 开始时间

        - XXX is to start on

      - 持续时间

        - last for ……

    - 参与者

      - XXX are all welcome to attend the camp

  - 参加原因

    - I want to take part in XXX because of my keen interest in XXX.

    - I have seen XXX is really XXX. and XXX will give me a chance to learn how to XXX.

    - XXX is a part of XX culture/problem,so I want know more about it.

  - 对对方好处

    - 帮助了解XX文化

    - 提供学习XX的机会

    - 你肯定很喜欢/感兴趣

  - 交际

    - 对上位者

      - We will be pleased if you could honor us with your presence.

- 申请

  - 写作目的

    - I have learned that XXX is recruiting XXX.

    - I'm writing to apply for this position.

  - 基本信息

    - I'm a Senior 3 student in Hongxing Middle school, and I believe I'm qualified for the position.

  - 原因

    - 做出贡献

      - I take it as my duty to contribute to our society.

    - 传播文化

      - as a culture ambassador spread Chinese  culture

    - 开拓视野

    - 广交朋友

    - 参与盛事

    - 热爱XX

  - 优势

    - 细节＋品质

    - 英语口语好

      - I hava been on several exchange programs in America, so I have a good command of oral English.

    - 知识底蕴强

      - As a fan of XXX history/culture, I'm sure my extensive knowledge will prove helpful when I XXX.

  - 表示期望

    - I will appreciate it if you coul offer me the chance to work for you. Thank you for your consideration.

  - 交际

- 道歉

  - 原计划

  - 表达歉意

    - I'm terribly sorry to tell you that I can't go to Yunnan with you as previously scheduled.

    - Please accept my sincere apology  for not being able to go to Yunnan this July.

  - 原因

  - 另约时间

- 征稿

  - 稿件要求

  - 投稿时间和方式

  - 询问意向

- 求助

  - 请求

    - Could you please do me a favor?

- 祝贺

- 建议

- 感谢

- 谈个人看法
